## Rust crates

| Path | Owns | Do not put here |
|---|---|---|
| `crates/commol-core/src/types/` | The model data types shared by all crates and serialized from Python: `Model` (header, `engine`, `process`, `structure`), `Process` (`process.rs`: bins, accumulators, stratifications, transitions; the names `NEIGHBORS` and `NEIGHBORS_PREFIX`), `ModelStructure` (one variant per kind, tagged by `kind`), `Aggregate` (`aggregate.rs`: `InitialConditions`, `Neighbors` and their checks, `AggregateVariant`), `Transition` (`transitions.rs`), …; `types/test_kind.rs` only with the `test-kind` feature. | Engine behavior. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/types/engine.rs` | `EngineType`, the one list of engines (model types): per entry, the model kinds it runs (`kinds`, `engines_of`; `ModelKind`, with the value types it accepts, `ModelValueType`), the form of its dynamics (`DynamicsForm`: its schedule and the left-hand side of its equations), its settings and their validation, the meaning of a rate, and the positions it reports at (`reports_at_integer_steps`, `check_position`, the one rule every engine and the builder read). `ModelEngine`, the engine and settings of a model. | The engines themselves. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/position.rs` | When two positions on the axis are one (`same_position`, `POSITION_TOLERANCE`), for every crate. | A tolerance of positions of its own. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/parallelism.rs` | `Parallelism` (a number of threads and the `Levels` that run in parallel), the `rayon` pool of each setting (`install`), and `inner_levels_in_parallel`, which a nested level reads. | A pool of its own elsewhere; the global pool of `rayon`. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/math_expression/` | The language of formulas (`language.rs`: `FUNCTIONS` and the check of a formula, `MathExpression::check`), the engine variables (`variables.rs`); the parser of formulas and its limits (`jit/parser.rs`: `MAX_NESTING`, `MAX_NODES`), the cut of a formula that a message quotes (`math_expression/error.rs`: `quoted`), the tree of a formula (`jit/ast.rs`) and its JSON, the port through which Python reads it (`tree.rs`: `MathExpression::tree_json`), the Cranelift JIT and its global cache (`jit/`). | Model-specific variables. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/simulation_engine.rs` | The `SimulationEngine` trait, for an engine of any kind: runs at positions (`check_position`), outputs (`output_names`, `state_outputs`, `output_sums`), parameters, reset, the seed of a stochastic engine (`set_seed`), the entry of the list of engines it runs and whether it draws random numbers (`engine_type`, `is_stochastic`), and the values of its kind (`plan_values`, `apply_values`, `ValuePlan`); `Trajectory`, `check_positions`, `run_replicates` (one run per seed, in parallel); the rules `NUM_STEPS`, `REPLICATE_COUNT` and `SEED` (a whole number from 0 to 2^64 - 1, the one rule of every seed). What calibration needs from any engine. | Code for one engine or one kind. |
| `crates/commol-core/src/range.rs` | The range rules of the settings, as data: `Count` (a whole number, at least a minimum or in an interval, read from a signed number with one message for every value outside), `Real` with its two `Bound`s (open or closed; NaN is always outside), `Rule`, the rules of one object of the Python API (`Ranges`, whose `check` reads each setting as a `Setting` of its own type: a whole number, a number, a list or not a number) and `check_object`, which finds the object in the tables of the crates; `CONFIDENCE_LEVEL`, the one rule of a confidence level, which each caller checks at its boundary, and the table of the replicates of a simulation and of the axis patterns (`RANGES`). | A rule of a setting written as a check of its own; a message of a range elsewhere. |
| `crates/commol-random/` | What every stochastic engine and inference method shares and no model: the generator (`Generator`, `Xoshiro256PlusPlus` seeded by SplitMix64, with its uniforms and the generator trait of `rand_core`, which the particle swarm draws with), `replicate_seed`, `seed_or_drawn` (the one place a seed that its caller does not set is drawn), `Stream` (`streams.rs`), the one registry of the seed streams of every method that draws from a seed, the starts of a calibration and the inference methods: each stream with its own number, its method and its purpose (`Stream::ALL`), and the one scheme of a draw, seed, then `Stream::seed`, then `Stream::replicate` for each draw or replicate, the samplers (`sample`: exponential, Poisson, binomial, multinomial, normal, gamma, an index below `n`), `SumTree`, the rates of a set of events, which the stochastic engine of the test kind uses (`StochasticEvents` scans its propensities), and `latin_hypercube` on the unit cube. | Anything of a model or an engine; a sampler in another crate; a stream number written in a method. |
| `crates/commol-statistics/src/statistics.rs` | The one quantile (weighted, midpoints of the cumulative weights; Hazen with equal weights), used by the summary of the replicates of a calibration and by every interval, sample and band of `commol-inference` and by the bindings, which import it from this crate; weighted mean and covariance, the weighted summary of a column (`summary`: mean, median, central interval, minimum and maximum, the reduction of every band and of every summary of runs), Kish's effective size; the two quantiles of a central interval (`interval_levels`, a pure function: the caller checks the level with `commol_core::CONFIDENCE_LEVEL`) and the check of quantile levels (`check_levels`). It depends on no crate of commol. | Quantiles or summaries anywhere else. |
| `crates/commol-statistics/src/numeric.rs` | The numerical helpers that the calibration and the inference methods share, one copy of each: `log_sum_exp`, `x_log_y`, `x_log1p_y`, `ln_gamma`, `ln_beta`, `digamma`, `trigamma`, and the log densities of the normal, the log-normal and the Student t in one or several dimensions. | A second copy of a helper or a density; anything of a model. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/builder.rs` | Expansion of the bins and accumulators of a process over its stratifications (`expand`, `expand_compartments`, `expand_accumulators`), the initial state (`initial_state`), the checks of an aggregate model (`check_model`: variant, neighbors, names, whole units for a stochastic engine; `as_whole`), the subgroup total names (`subgroup_total_names`), impulse schedules and the compilation into `CompiledModel` (`try_from_model`). | Flow planning; stepping. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/layout.rs` | `CompartmentLayout`: compartments in engine order with their labels, and lookups by bin and categories. | Name parsing. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/labels.rs` | `OutputLabels` (bin and categories of an output) and `output_sums` (bin totals and partial totals). | Name parsing. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/engine.rs` | `CompartmentalEngine`: what a compartmental engine gives besides `SimulationEngine`: its compiled model (`model`, `model_mut`), and from it by default `compartments`, `output_labels`, `initial_state` and `total`; `set_initial_condition`. What the four engines share: `delegate_to_compiled_model!` (the methods of `SimulationEngine` that read the compiled model), `IntegerStepper` and `record_steps` (the run of an engine that reports only at integer steps), `rounded` (the numbers of a message). | Code for one engine. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/initial_conditions.rs` | How initial-condition values reach a compartmental engine (`InitialConditionPlan`: resolution, layers, remainder); the `plan_values` and `apply_values` of every engine of the aggregate kind use it. | Parameter values. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/flows.rs` | Transition flow planning (`plan_transition_flows`), the sums the neighbors of a unit are (`resolve_neighbors`), and the description of the flows for Python (`describe_flows`). | Rate evaluation at run time. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/helpers.rs` | Stratified-rate resolution and rate rewriting helpers. | New public API. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/rates.rs` | Evaluation of a compiled rate: fast path or JIT with resolved slots; an error names the transition. | Flow logic. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/rules.rs` | The rule of each value of a model, declared once: an initial amount (`check_bin_amount`, `check_compartment_amount`), the fractions of a stratification (`check_fractions`), an impulse (`check_impulse`) and the points of a series (`check_series`). The compiler and the Python object of the value call them. | A check of a value written in the compiler or in Python. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/system.rs` | The flows every engine reads: `flow_rates` (the value of each flow), `transition_rates` (its rate), `flow_changes` (the change of one event of each flow), the right-hand side `derivatives` built on them; impulses (`apply_impulses`, `impulse_rates`, `impulse_changes`, `impulse_positions`); the positions where a rate can jump (`breakpoints`, `reads_time`); the parameters a caller sets (`set_parameter`, `check_parameter`: a constant or a parameter to calibrate); the accessors the engines use. | Stepping along the axis. |
| `crates/commol-model/src/types.rs` | `CompiledModel`, compiled flows, `FlowChange` (one event of a flow), impulse schedules, series lookup (`SeriesParameter`). | Python types. |
| `crates/commol-difference/src/simulation.rs` | `DifferenceEquations`: the discrete step (`state += derivatives`), impulses at integer steps, and its `IntegerStepper`, `SimulationEngine` and `CompartmentalEngine` implementations. | Model compilation. |
| `crates/commol-differential/src/lib.rs` | `DifferentialEquations`: state, output positions, impulses, and its `SimulationEngine` and `CompartmentalEngine` implementations. | Numerical methods. |
| `crates/commol-differential/src/solve.rs` | The integration with `diffsol`: pieces between the breakpoints of the model, the finite-difference `J·v`, dense or sparse matrices (`SPARSE_THRESHOLD`). | Model compilation. |
| `crates/commol-stochastic/src/events.rs` | `StochasticEvents`: whole units one event at a time (the direct method, with a time change when a rate reads `t`) or in leaps (tau-leaping, fixed or chosen as Cao, Gillespie and Petzold 2006 do), stopping at the breakpoints; its `SimulationEngine` and `CompartmentalEngine` implementations. | Samplers; flows. |
| `crates/commol-stochastic/src/steps.rs` | `StochasticSteps`: the chain binomial (one multinomial per source, capped Poisson draws for absolute flows), the check of its per-step probabilities, and its `IntegerStepper`, `SimulationEngine` and `CompartmentalEngine` implementations. | Samplers; flows. |
| `crates/commol-stochastic/src/units.rs` | What both stochastic engines share: the generator, the whole start of a run (`largest_remainder`, `random_rounding`) and impulses that move whole units. | Anything of one engine. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/calibration_problem.rs` | `CalibrationProblem`: the layers below, built once (`new`, which refuses observation models with a stochastic engine; `with_replicates`; `with_replicate_settings`, the replicates, seed and summary a stochastic engine needs, with the message that names a missing one; `with_observation_normalization`, `with_initial_condition_remainder`), a copy with other observed values and weights (`with_data`), a copy whose runs also report at the positions of other observations (`observing`), the one evaluation of a point (`evaluate`), the predictions of one seeded run (`predict_once`), and the position check that names an observation or constraint (`check_positions`). It uses only `SimulationEngine`. | Penalties; optimizer setup; anything of one model kind. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/forward.rs` | `ForwardModel`: every clone, reset, write of values and run of the engine (`run_schedule`, `run_observed`, `run_at`), one run per replicate of a stochastic engine, or one run seeded by its caller (`run_once`); the same model on other observations (`with_observations`, which checks their positions), or with none (`unobserved`, which asks a stochastic engine for replicates and a seed); `Workspace`; the one schedule of a set of observations (`schedule_of`: each position and window start, sorted, once) and the one mean over replicates (`replicate_mean`). | Loss, penalties, history; a second schedule or mean over replicates. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/series.rs` | `SeriesIndex`: the one index of the series of a set of observations (or of labels): the labels in order of first appearance, and the entries of each in position order. Every method that reads the observations series by series uses it. | A second grouping of observations by label. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/observation.rs` | `ObservationOperator`: one prediction per observation from the rows of a run (rows, sums of outputs, windows, factors); `index_at`, the row of a position within the tolerance of the axis; the arithmetic of every window: `window_start` and `check_window_end`, the window of an observation; `window_increments`, which `windowed_totals` also reads; the grid of windows that results, figures and reports read (`window_bounds`, `window_index`, `window_ends`, `windows_in`). | Comparison with data. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/feasibility.rs` | `Feasibility` (bounds, constraints), `Violation` and `EvaluationError`. | Penalties. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/discrepancy.rs` | `Discrepancy` and `DiscrepancyConfig`: the loss, or the negative log-likelihood of observation models, of predictions against the data, with weights, per-series normalization of a loss and the summary of the replicates; the discrepancy of one run (`of_run`); the base of the penalty (`penalty_base`). | Running engines. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/likelihood/` | The observation models: `ObservationModel` and `Argument` (`likelihood/mod.rs`), `Likelihood` (the model and trials of each observation, the checks of models, noise parameters and observed values, the pointwise log-likelihood), `Noise` (`density.rs`: a model with numbers for its noise, its log density, the means it allows, its scores and Fisher information), `TermDerivatives` (the gradient and the expected curvature of each observation's term, also of a loss through `Discrepancy::term_derivatives`). | Draws; running engines. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/objective.rs` | `OptimizationObjective`: the argmin cost function, the only place that turns a violation into a penalty; `score_all`, the points of a swarm in parallel and in order; the history decorator (`Recorded`). | Recording history inside the objective. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/starts.rs` | The starts of a multi-start optimization (`start_points`: the guess, then a Latin hypercube) and `optimize_starts`, the one function that runs them in parallel and chooses the best (`Starts`), for a calibration and every fit of a replicate. | Statistics. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/ranges.rs` | The range rule of each setting of a calibration (observations, constraints, observation models, optimizers, the problem) and the table `RANGES` of their objects of the Python API. The functions and constructors that take a setting check it with its rule (`ObservedDataPoint::check_ranges`, `NelderMeadConfig::check_ranges`, …). | A check of a range written in a function. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/types.rs` | `LossConfig` (every loss, and `replicate_loss`), `ReplicateSummary` (its median and quantile by `statistics::quantiles`), `crps` and `weighted_crps`, `Replicates`, the observation, parameter, constraint and result types. | Optimizer setup. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/values.rs` | How calibrated values reach any engine: parameters by id, which the engine checks when a problem is built (`set_parameter` on a copy), values of the model kind through the engine (`ValueWriter`, `check_model_values`, `configured_engine`); a factor has positive bounds (`ValueWriter::new`). | How a kind resolves its values; a check of parameter ids in Python. |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/optimization.rs` | Nelder-Mead and particle swarm setup on the argmin fork, from a start point and the seed of a swarm (`optimize`, `optimize_from`, `optimize_with_history`), and on a subproblem (`optimize_subproblem`: a smaller search space, or the augmented Lagrangian of the fork around the problem's optimizer); no output to the process streams. | Loss definitions; numerical methods that know no model, such as the sine transform of the bounds or the handling of a NaN cost by the swarm (the argmin fork). |
| `crates/commol-calibration/src/subproblem.rs` | `Subproblem`: the problem with one parameter held (left out of the search) or one prediction held (an equality constraint, `ConstraintSettings`); `predict`, the value of a prediction; `SubproblemResult`. | Stepping along a profile; thresholds. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/ranges.rs` | The range rule of each setting of the inference methods, the distributions and the quantities of interest, and the table `RANGES` of their objects of the Python API; the method that takes a setting checks it with its rule. | A check of a range written in a method. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/error.rs` | `MethodError`, the one error of every method, and its `ErrorKind` (`Invalid` or `Failed`), which sets the Python exception. | An error type of one method. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/sample.rs` | `ParameterSample`: weighted draws, their invariants and statistics, resampling. | What a method does. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/predictive.rs` | `Predictive` (the run of every draw, its bands), `observation_draws` through `ForwardModel::with_observations`, the `ObservationNoise` port (one draw's whole vector), `NoNoise`, and `DistributionNoise` with the draw of each observation model; `Band`, the one layout of a band (`[position][output]`, also of the delta method) and its one reducer (`Band::of`, on `statistics::summary`); `share_among_replicates`, the one share of the weight of a draw among its replicates. | Noise models of one method; a second band reducer or layout. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/resampling/` | Fits to reweighted or resampled data: `blocks.rs` (`BlockScheme`, the automatic block length, and the one resolution of a block length, given or automatic, with its estimates: `BlockScheme::resolve`, `BlockScheme::at_fit`, which the sandwich, the bootstraps, the jackknife, the calibration of `η` and the conformal methods call), `residuals.rs` (`Residuals` on a scale, residuals by series and position), `resamplers.rs` (the `Resampler` port, `Perturbation`, the Bayesian, residual and wild bootstraps, `ParametricResampler`), `runner.rs` (`ReplicateRunner` and its one loop `fit_all`, `ReplicateFits`, `FitUnit`, `bootstrap`), `jackknife.rs` (`block_jackknife`), `noise.rs` (`ResidualNoise`). | Changes to the problem other than through `with_data`. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/conformal/` | Conformal prediction intervals: `score.rs` (`NonconformityScore`, `Pooling`, `ConformalSettings`, the scale of a score), `quantile.rs` (the conformal quantile, the order statistics of jackknife+), `intervals.rs` (`PredictionIntervals`, `ConformalRun`), `predict.rs` (predictions and spread at any observations, the target points), and one file per variant: `split.rs`, `rolling.rs`, `jackknife_plus.rs` (with `cv_plus_bound`), `enbpi.rs`, `adaptive.rs` (`AdaptiveLevel`). | A second quantile or score of one variant. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/scores.rs` | Coverage, interval score, weighted interval score, sample CRPS, PIT, the log score of observation models, and their means per series. | — |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/sensitivity/` | `SensitivitySource`, the port of derivatives of the predicted observations; `FiniteDifferences` on the design of the observations or a dense one; the scaled sensitivities and `observation_scales`. | Ranks, verdicts. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/design.rs`, `crates/commol-inference/src/quantity.rs`, `batch.rs` | What a design-based method shares: `InputSpace` (independent distributions or a sample) and its designs (Latin hypercube, Morris trajectories, the Saltelli matrices, the scale `F(x)`); `Series`, `Quantity` (a series and its reduction, read from JSON) and `Quantities`, read through the observation operator, with the schedule of a list of quantities and the quantities of a run that is done (`evaluate_run`); `BatchRunner`, every point through a forward model in parallel, replicate by replicate or as their mean, which rejects a quantity that is NaN. | Estimators. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/global_sensitivity/` | `analysis.rs` (`ForwardAnalysis`: the forward model, the inputs and the seed streams, and each method in one call; with no seed it draws one, which a stochastic engine refuses), and the estimators: `prcc.rs` (weighted PRCC and its test), `morris.rs`, `sobol.rs` (with the bootstrap of rows), `chaos.rs` (`ChaosExpansion`, a surrogate, and `Truncation`, the terms its basis keeps). | Designs; code of one engine. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/identifiability/` | The analyses: `structural_rank` (ranks, null space, combinations), `collinearity` (Brun's indices), `sample_identifiability` (contraction, correlations, modes), `starts_identifiability`. | Derivatives; reports. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/profiles/` | Profile likelihood: `tracer.rs` (`ProfileTracer`, the adaptive step and the secant start, `Profile` and its interval), `threshold.rs` (`chi_square`, `beale`, `bootstrap_thresholds`), `verdict.rs` (`ProfileVerdict`), `resets.rs` (`read_structure`: the resets of a flat profile, `StructuralReading`). | Holding a quantity (a `Subproblem`'s); optimizers. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/target.rs` | `Target` (a log density, `−∞` outside the support) and `discrepancy_in_support`. | Penalties. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/distribution.rs`, `special.rs` | `Family` and `Distribution`, one family truncated to the bounds of a parameter (density, distribution function, quantile, closed-form moments, draws by inversion); the normal distribution function and its inverse, the regularized incomplete gamma and beta functions and the quantiles of the Student t and the F (`special.rs`, on `libm`). The quantile sets the bracket of the root and takes the root finder from the argmin fork (`NewtonRoot`). | Distributions from a dependency, whose draws change with it; a root finder of its own. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/priors.rs` | `Priors`: one distribution per parameter, the check of their bounds, the joint density and draws. | Defaults of a prior. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/transform.rs` | `Transform`: the unbounded scale a move proposes on (logit, log or identity by the bounds) and its log Jacobian; `on_bound` and `ON_BOUND`, the one rule of a value on a bound, which the curvature, the Laplace mixture and the profiles read. | Kernels; a second rule of a bound. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/local/` | Local approximations at a fit: `Curvature` (Gauss–Newton or the stepped Hessian of the `finitediff` crate of the argmin fork, on the unbounded scale, with the term gradients), `CurvatureKind` and `laplace_approximation` (`approximation.rs`), `Gaussian` and `laplace` (`gaussian.rs`), `delta_band` (`band.rs`), `sandwich`, `block_meat` and `sandwich_approximation` (`sandwich.rs`), `laplace_mixture` (`mixture.rs`). | Finite-difference formulas of their own; samplers. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/psis.rs`, `importance.rs` | Pareto-smoothed importance sampling (`smooth`, `k̂`); `importance_sample` of a `Target` from a `Proposal` on the unbounded scale, and `TemperedPosterior`, the last level of a `TemperedTarget` as a `Target`, which runs a stochastic model with one replicate of its seed (`Stream::PosteriorReplicate`) at every point. | Proposals of one method. |
| `crates/commol-inference/src/smc/` | Sequential Monte Carlo: the `TemperedTarget` port (`smc/mod.rs`), `SmcSampler`, `next_level` and `abc_rejection` (`sampler.rs`), the `Kernel` port and `RandomWalk` (`kernel.rs`), `GibbsTarget` with the calibrations of `η` (`gibbs.rs`), `AbcTarget` with its distances (`abc.rs`), `LikelihoodTarget` and `bayesian_posterior` (`likelihood.rs`). | Anything of one target in the sampler. |

## PyO3 bindings

| Path | Owns | Do not put here |
|---|---|---|
| `py-commol/build.rs`, `py-commol/src/build_info.rs` | The version and the commit of a build: the build script reads the commit from the git metadata of the source and writes `COMMOL_VERSION` and `COMMOL_COMMIT`, which `py-commol/src/lib.rs` gives as `__version__` and `__commit__` of the extension; `build_info.rs` holds the rule (the placeholder `0.1.0` is not a release) and its tests. | Anything that runs the `git` program. |
| `py-commol/src/json.rs` | The port of the bridge: `read`, which reads the JSON of the `model_dump()` of a data object into the type of the engine, with the path of the field an error is about, and the module `json` of `commol_rs`: `as_read`, which reads an object by the name of its Python class and writes back what it read, and `objects`, the classes it reads. | A conversion of a field. |
| `py-commol/src/core.rs` | The reading of a model, a process and an aggregate structure from their JSON, `MathExpression`, and the range check of `py-commol/src/ranges.rs`. | Logic. |
| `py-commol/src/counts.rs` | `count` and `optional_count`, the one reading of a count or a seed a binding takes from Python: the binding takes a count as `i64` and a seed as `i128` (its range ends at 2^64 - 1), so PyO3 reads only the type, and the rule of the setting in the engine converts it, with its message for a value outside the range. | A message or a minimum of its own. |
| `py-commol/src/ranges.rs` | `commol_rs.core.check_ranges`, the one check of the range rules of an object of the Python API, which joins the tables `RANGES` of `commol-core`, `commol-calibration` and `commol-inference`, and `range_rules`, each rule with a value outside its range and its message, for the tests. | A rule of a setting (the crates'). |
| `py-commol/src/model.rs` | The queries of `commol_rs.model` on a process and its aggregate structure: `initial_state`, `expanded_outputs`, `transition_flows`, `subgroup_total_names`; `check_model` on a model; the rules of the values of a model (`check_bin_amounts`, `check_compartment_amounts`, `check_fractions`, `check_impulse`, `check_series`); the names `NEIGHBORS` and `NEIGHBORS_PREFIX`. | Logic beyond type conversion. |
| `py-commol/src/engine.rs` | `commol_rs.engine`: `Engine::build` (the four engines of the aggregate kind and those of the test kind), which checks the kind and builds the implementation of the pair of an `EngineType` entry and the kind of the structure, and the Python classes `Engine` (any engine, built from a model), `EngineType` (the list, for Python to ask) and `ModelKind`. | Engine logic. |
| `py-commol/src/test_kind.rs` | `TestEngine` and `TestStochasticEngine`, the engines of the test model kind, and `commol_rs.test_kind.threads_of_runs`, the threads that ran them; only with the `test-kind` feature. | Anything a release needs. |
| `py-commol/src/calibration.rs` | The results of a fit (`CalibrationResult`, `Starts`, the history), the checks that take an `Engine` and the parameters as JSON, and the windows of the engine (`check_window`, `check_window_end`, `window_bounds`, `window_index`, `window_ends`, `windows_in`, `window_increments`). | Logic beyond type conversion. |
| `py-commol/src/identifiability.rs` | `commol_rs.inference`: `structural_rank`, `collinearity`, `sample_identifiability`, `starts_identifiability`, each with the JSON of its method and the handle of the result it reads, the checks of the engine type and of the names, and their result classes. | Logic beyond type conversion. |
| `py-commol/src/smc.rs` | `commol_rs.inference`: `Distribution`, the runners of the sequential Monte Carlo methods (`gibbs_posterior`, which fits the problem and calibrates `η` when its method asks, `bayesian_posterior`, `abc_smc`, `abc_rejection`), each with the JSON of its method, and `SMCRun`, `EtaCalibration`. | Logic beyond type conversion. |

## Placement rule

Place code at the narrowest owner that can hold it:

1. **A rule about how a model becomes numbers** → a Rust crate.
2. **Type conversion between Python and Rust** → `py-commol/src/`.
3. **Validation of user input** → the Pydantic model in `context/`.
4. **User-facing workflow** → `api/`.
5. **Presentation** (reports, figures) → `api/reporting.py`, `api/plotting/`.
