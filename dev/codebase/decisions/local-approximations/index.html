    Local approximations¶

    Back to Design decisions

    How the curvature, the Laplace approximation, the sandwich and the Laplace mixture read a fit, and where their scores, their Fisher information and PSIS live.

    The curvature lives on the unbounded scale¶

    Curvature (crates/commol-inference/src/local/curvature.rs) holds H_u = D · H_θ · D, with D = dθ/du at the fit on the scale of Transform (transform.rs: a logit with two bounds, a log with one, the identity with none), centred on the fit mapped to that scale, with no curvature of the log Jacobian. Every draw maps back with from_unbounded, so it lies inside the bounds; every density on that scale, as the importance weights of the mixture, adds log_jacobian. A fit on a bound, within 1e-8 of the width of its bounds (on_bound), is refused with a message that names the parameter: its variance on that scale is infinite. The tolerance comes from the optimizer: Nelder-Mead searches on a sine (SineTransform) of the unbounded coordinate, which approaches a bound quadratically in u and ends about 1e-15 of the width from it; 1e-8 of the width is a step δu ≈ 2e-4, far below any posterior spread. The mixture skips such an end and notes it; when the end fits at least as well as the worst kept component (its objective is not above), the mixture misses an optimum the data support, and the sample is marked unreliable, since k̂ cannot see mass the proposal never reaches.

    Alternative Why not
    The scale of the parameters A normal there puts mass outside the bounds near them.
    A truncated normal on the scale of the parameters It biases the mean and breaks the delta method.
    Centring on the mode of the density on the unbounded scale, Jacobian included It moves the centre away from the fit for no gain: importance sampling corrects the difference.
    A fit on a bound by exact equality An optimizer that the bound stops ends a few roundings from it, where the variance on the unbounded scale is huge but finite.
    Every skipped end marks the sample unreliable An end on a bound that fits worse than every component holds little mass; the note is enough.

    Guards: crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::draws_mapped_back_stay_inside_the_bounds, crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::an_end_on_a_bound_better_than_a_component_marks_the_sample_unreliable, crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::an_end_on_a_bound_worse_than_every_component_is_only_noted, tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestCurvature::test_a_fit_on_a_bound_is_refused, tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestResults::test_an_end_on_a_bound_gives_no_component.

    Gauss–Newton by default, the Hessian on request¶

    Curvature::gauss_newton reads the expected curvature of each observation's term over its arguments, the mean and the noise arguments of its model (Noise::information), through the Jacobian of SensitivitySource and the identity of each noise parameter: Jᵀ W J on the model parameters, with the cross terms where they do not vanish (a log-normal of a Mean center, the two arguments of a Student t). Curvature::hessian takes the central Hessian of the objective with given steps of the finitediff crate of the argmin fork, with steps δ^{1/4} · dθ/du (δ^{1/4} · max(|θ|, 1) with no bound), δ the output accuracy of the engine, so θ ± 2h stays inside the bounds. Each entry of either carries an estimated error; an entry above 1 % of √(H_jj H_kk) is a note. The threshold measures the error on the scale of a correlation, so an off-diagonal entry near 0 is judged against its row and column; a note changes no result.

    Alternative Why not
    The Hessian always 4p² + 1 runs, and indefinite away from the optimum.
    Gauss–Newton always Wrong when the residuals are large or the mean is wrong, where the observed and expected curvature differ.
    The fixed step √(2ε) of finitediff With an ODE solved to rtol, an error of about accuracy / h².
    Differences written in commol The fork keeps the numerical methods; commol chooses the steps from its engine.

    Guards: crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::curvatures_of_a_linear_model_equal_the_information, crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::the_hessian_of_a_linear_model_equals_the_information, crates/commol-calibration/tests/test_likelihood.rs::fisher_information_of_each_model_has_its_closed_form.

    Scores and information live in commol-calibration¶

    Discrepancy::term_derivatives (crates/commol-calibration/src/discrepancy.rs) gives each observation's gradient and expected curvature over its arguments: −2 w n² r and 2 w n² for the sum of squares, the same times c = 1 / (2 √(W · S)) for the root mean squared error (its derivative per unit of S), −w times Noise::scores and w times Noise::information for an observation model. The mean absolute error is refused: its sandwich needs a density of the residuals at 0 and a bandwidth. The sandwich, information matching and later gradient methods read them.

    Alternative Why not
    Derivatives in each method Every method would repeat the models and their noise arguments.
    A kernel density for the absolute error It hides a bandwidth choice; the bootstrap needs none.

    Guards: crates/commol-calibration/tests/test_likelihood.rs::scores_equal_the_differences_of_the_terms.

    The sandwich sums scores over blocks¶

    sandwich (local/sandwich.rs) gives H⁻¹ B H⁻¹ with B = G/(G − 1) · Σ_b s_b s_bᵀ over the non-overlapping blocks of BlockScheme (CR1), and its draws and intervals read a Student t with G − 1 degrees of freedom. G ≤ p is refused, since the block scores of a fit sum to zero and B would have rank below p. block_length has no default; "auto" is the length of non-overlapping blocks of the residuals. Information matching (smc/gibbs.rs) reads the same H as J and the same B as I, so it takes a likelihood and holds under dependence.

    Alternative Why not
    The independent sandwich (White) Too narrow when the residuals are autocorrelated.
    Newey–West It needs lags across series at irregular positions, and does not reuse the blocks.
    A warning below a fixed number of blocks An arbitrary threshold; the t widens the intervals by the right amount.
    CR2 with Bell–McCaffrey degrees of freedom Linear theory and the leverage of each block; kept for when a coverage study needs it.

    Guards: tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestSandwich, tests/uncertainty/test_coverage.py::TestSandwich, tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestInformationMatching.

    The Laplace approximation needs observation models¶

    laplace_approximation (local/approximation.rs) refuses a loss: a loss has no noise scale, so its curvature is no inverse variance. The message names NormalObservations(sd="sd") with a noise parameter, whose fit estimates the scale by maximum likelihood (SSE/n, narrower than SSE/(n − p) by √(n/(n − p))), and the sandwich, which needs no noise model. A result holds only data: Calibrator.delta_band(result, positions, level) reads the problem from the calibrator, so a result read back from JSON gives the same band.

    Alternative Why not
    A variance scale SSE / (n − p) It assumes normal, independent noise of one size, which a loss does not state.
    LaplaceResult.delta_band with a private reference to the problem A result read back from JSON has none.

    Guards: tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestLaplace::test_laplace_refuses_a_loss, tests/uncertainty/test_local.py::TestLaplace::test_a_result_read_from_json_gives_the_same_delta_band.

    PSIS lives in commol-inference¶

    smooth (crates/commol-inference/src/psis.rs) follows Vehtari et al. (2024) as ArviZ does: the tail of ⌊3√S⌋ or ⌊S/5⌋ weights, the generalized Pareto fit of Zhang and Stephens (2009) with a prior on k, the quantiles at (r + ½)/M kept at most the largest weight. Its test compares k̂ and the smoothed weights with values computed once with ArviZ and stored in the test. importance_sample (importance.rs) takes any Proposal and any Target; the mixture is one proposal.

    Alternative Why not
    ArviZ A large Python dependency for one function, and the weights would leave Rust and come back.
    The argmin fork PSIS is a statistical estimator, not optimization.
    A crate of its own Every reader is in commol-inference; no Rust crate implements it.

    Guards: crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::psis_matches_the_reference_values, crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::banana_target_gives_a_large_k_hat, crates/commol-inference/tests/test_local.rs::mixture_finds_both_modes_in_proportion.
