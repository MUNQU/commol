    Initial conditions¶

    Back to Design decisions

    How the fractions of the categories compose, and how calibrated values reach the initial state. The initial state itself is an amount per bin (Model structure).

    Category fractions compose down the condition chain¶

    The fractions of a conditional stratification are relative to the subgroup that its conditions select, not to the whole bin. The amount of a compartment therefore multiplies along the chain: bin amount × fraction(group1) × fraction(cat1) (initial_state in crates/commol-model/src/builder.rs). Aggregate.subgroup_total sums the state the engine builds, overrides included, and Aggregate.get_conditioning_categories names the subgroup (context/aggregate.py).

    Guard: tests/simulation/test_outputs.py::TestSubgroupTotal compares the result with the state the engine starts from.

    Calibrated initial conditions are applied in layers¶

    InitialConditionPlan in crates/commol-model/src/initial_conditions.rs applies calibrated values in a fixed order: bins, category splits, exact compartments, and then the remainder named by CalibrationProblem.initial_condition_remainder. A bin, prefix or compartment value is an amount in the unit of its bin; a category value is a split. Both engines of the aggregate kind resolve and write initial conditions through it (SimulationEngine::plan_values and apply_values), and the calibration writes every value through the engine (ValueWriter in crates/commol-calibration/src/values.rs), so the objective, the predictions and the write-back (configured_engine, then the binding calibrated_initial_state) agree by construction. Aggregate.with_calibrated_values (context/_model/calibration_write_back.py) writes back the amount of every bin whose every compartment a value or the remainder sets and the split of every calibrated category, and pins as per-compartment overrides every compartment that these cannot reproduce.

    Rule: the plan reads the initial state of the engine (CompartmentalEngine::initial_state), never its current state: the split of a bin, a prefix or the remainder and the pair totals of a category. A Calibrator takes the engine of its Simulation, which holds the last state of a run.

    Guards: crates/commol-model/tests/initial_conditions.rs::a_bin_and_the_remainder_keep_the_split_of_the_initial_state_after_a_run, tests/calibration/test_initial_conditions.py::test_a_calibrator_on_a_simulation_that_has_run_splits_a_bin_by_the_initial_state.

    The remainder keeps the sum the model declares¶

    The remainder takes the sum of the amounts the model states (CompartmentalEngine::total, fixed when the engine is built), less every other compartment after the values are written. A compartment that no value covers keeps its declared amount and cancels out, so the remainder keeps the declared sum of the compartments the values and it cover, and a bin of another unit does not enter. A bin left None counts as 0, so the model states the sum on the remainder ({"A": 1000, "B": None}). Without a remainder every state is feasible, because the engine rejects a negative amount. With one, a remainder below zero is infeasible by its share of the amounts the values set, a number with no unit that is never a division by zero. ForwardModel::configure (forward.rs) puts the message of the plan of values (ValuePlan::infeasibility) in the Violation::InfeasibleStart it returns, so a calibration (CalibrationProblem::configured_engine) and a predictive draw (prediction_error in py-commol/src/inference.rs) give the same message, and the binding needs no access to the problem (tests/uncertainty/test_predictive.py::TestBands::test_a_draw_with_an_infeasible_start_names_its_set).

    Units live only in Python (the Rust Bin has none), so Aggregate.check_calibration_values rejects a remainder whose unit is not the unit of every compartment a value sets, from the coverage the engine resolves (InitialConditionPlan::coverage, the binding initial_condition_coverage).

    Alternative Why not
    A total in the problem that the remainder keeps It repeats a number of the model and can contradict it.
    The sum of the bins of the unit of the remainder The engine has no units, and the unit of a bin is optional free text.
    The deficit as a share of the sum the remainder keeps That sum can be 0, as when every bin starts empty.

    Guards: tests/calibration/test_write_back.py::TestApplyCalibrationParameters::test_applied_model_reproduces_the_loss_the_optimizer_scored, tests/calibration/test_write_back.py::TestWriteBackReadability::test_an_amount_in_the_unit_of_its_bin_is_written_back, tests/calibration/test_initial_conditions.py::TestRemainderUnits; crates/commol-model/tests/initial_conditions.rs (the layers, the remainder and the targets of an id, and the_remainder_keeps_the_declared_sum_of_what_it_covers). See also Per-compartment initial conditions.
